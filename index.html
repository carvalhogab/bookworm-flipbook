Brand guidelines

A worm that turns a habit into a path.

Bookworm is a reading-habit tracker built around a streak, inspired by the way Strava and Duolingo keep people coming back.

The brand needed a character that felt playful, catchy and contemporary: friendly enough to cheer you on, simple enough to recognise at a glance.
